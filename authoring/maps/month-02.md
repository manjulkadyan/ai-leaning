## Week 5 lesson map: Calculus for machine learning
- Day 29 `day-29-derivatives.md` (~120 min): slope of a line → slope of a curve: secant lines shrinking to the tangent (animated SVG), the derivative as a limit explained without epsilon-delta, notation f'(x) and df/dx, derivative of x² from the definition, numerical derivative (forward vs central difference, and how the step size h trades truncation error against floating-point error — show the real error curve with a log-log plot), the derivative as "sensitivity: if I nudge x, how much does the output move". WIDGET: `tangent-explorer` (docs/javascripts/tangent-explorer.js): a plot of a few selectable functions (x², x³−x, sin x, sigmoid) with a draggable point showing the tangent line, the slope value and a secant with adjustable h. Android bridge: velocity from position samples (e.g. finite differences over SensorManager/touch MotionEvent positions, VelocityTracker). Next: Day 30: Derivative rules you'll actually use (coming soon).
- Day 30 `day-30-derivative-rules.md` (~90 min): constant, power, constant-multiple and sum rules; product rule; exp and ln; the derivatives ML uses every day: sigmoid σ'(x)=σ(x)(1−σ(x)), tanh, ReLU (and what happens at 0), softplus; a reference table; check every rule numerically with the Day 29 central difference; plot functions with their derivatives. Animated SVG e.g. ReLU vs sigmoid and their derivative shapes, vanishing gradient preview (sigmoid' ≤ 0.25). Next: Day 31: The chain rule and computational graphs (coming soon).
- Day 31 `day-31-chain-rule.md` (~120 min): composition of functions, the chain rule (gears analogy: rates multiply), computational graphs, forward pass then backward pass by hand on a small expression (e.g. f = (x·y + z)², then a neuron σ(w·x + b) with a squared error), local gradients multiply along a path and add across paths, animated SVG of gradients flowing backward through a graph, check against numerical derivatives, why this is backpropagation. Next: Day 32: Partial derivatives and the gradient (coming soon).
- Day 32 `day-32-partial-derivatives-gradient.md` (~120 min): functions of two variables as surfaces and contour maps (real matplotlib contour plots), partial derivatives (freeze the other variables), the gradient vector, it points uphill and is perpendicular to contours (figure with quiver arrows), directional derivative = ∇f·u, the gradient of the MSE of a 2-parameter line fit to a few points, a first "walk downhill" teaser (a few manual steps, no full GD yet — Day 51 does that). Animated SVG of a point stepping across contours. Next: Day 33: Gradients of vectors and matrices, checked numerically (coming soon).
- Day 33 `day-33-vector-matrix-gradients.md` (~120 min): gradient with respect to a vector, the shape rule (the gradient has the parameter's shape), d(w·x)/dw = x, d(‖w‖²)/dw = 2w, d(Wx)/dW = outer product, the MSE gradient for linear regression in matrix form ∇ = (2/n) Xᵀ(Xw − y) derived step by step and verified, numerical gradient checking with central differences and relative error (a reusable `grad_check(f, w)` function), the Jacobian in one paragraph, and the Play Store data (predict rating from a couple of standardized features) to compute a real gradient. Next: Day 34: Week 5 review and mini-project (coming soon).
- Day 34 `day-34-week5-review.md` (~3 h, review-day structure from brief3): recap of Days 29–33, self-check, re-do from blank; MINI-PROJECT: a tiny scalar autodiff engine — a `Value` class supporting +, −, *, /, ** (constant power), exp, tanh, relu, with `backward()` via topological sort; verify every op against numerical derivatives; then train a single neuron (or a 2-input logistic unit) on a toy dataset with it. Credit Andrej Karpathy's micrograd (https://github.com/karpathy/micrograd) and his video; say the Month 4 milestone builds a fuller version. Animated SVG summarising the week. Next: `**Next:** Week 6 begins with Day 36: Probability and random variables, by simulation (coming soon)` (Day 35 is rest).

## Week 6 lesson map: Probability
- Day 36 `day-36-probability-basics.md` (~90 min): outcomes, events, probability as long-run frequency, simulating with rng (coins, dice, app crash rates), rules (complement, addition, multiplication for independent events), random variables (discrete), probability mass function as a histogram that converges, the birthday problem by simulation and exactly. Animated SVG of a running frequency settling. Android bridge: e.g. crash-free sessions rate in Firebase Crashlytics, staged rollouts as probabilities. Next: Day 37: Common distributions: Bernoulli, binomial, uniform, normal (coming soon).
- Day 37 `day-37-distributions.md` (~120 min): Bernoulli, binomial (pmf formula with a worked example, simulate vs formula), uniform, normal (bell curve, μ and σ, the 68–95–99.7 rule, z-scores), continuous variables and probability density (area, not height), CDF; sampling each with rng and comparing histograms to formulas (real matplotlib figures); where distributions appear in ML (weight initialization, noise, output probabilities). Next: Day 38: Conditional probability and Bayes' rule (coming soon).
- Day 38 `day-38-bayes-rule.md` (~120 min): conditional probability with a 2×2 count table (from the Play Store data: e.g. P(rating ≥ 4.5 | has ads)), independence test, the multiplication rule, total probability, Bayes' rule derived from the table, the medical-test / spam-filter base-rate example worked by hand and by simulation (natural frequencies with an animated SVG of 1000 dots), prior, likelihood, posterior vocabulary. Android bridge e.g. on-device spam/notification classifier thresholds. Next: Day 39: Expectation, variance and the law of large numbers (coming soon).
- Day 39 `day-39-expectation-variance.md` (~90 min): expected value as probability-weighted average (dice, a game's in-app purchase value), linearity of expectation, variance and standard deviation, variance of a sum of independent variables, the law of large numbers by simulation (figure of running means), why averaging many noisy gradient estimates works (preview of SGD), sample mean vs true mean. Next: Day 40: Joint distributions, independence and the central limit theorem (coming soon).
- Day 40 `day-40-joint-clt.md` (~120 min): joint distributions as 2-D tables/histograms, marginals, conditional distributions, independence vs correlation (zero correlation ≠ independent example), covariance and correlation revisited (Day 24/25 link), the central limit theorem by simulation (sums of uniform/exponential/dice → normal; animated SVG of a histogram morphing), why CLT underpins confidence intervals (Week 7 preview), the multivariate normal in 2-D with its covariance ellipse (link to eigenvectors). Next: Day 41: Week 6 review and mini-project (coming soon).
- Day 41 `day-41-week6-review.md` (~3 h, review-day structure): recap of Days 36–40, self-check, re-do; MINI-PROJECT: a naive Bayes spam filter from scratch in NumPy on a small hand-written SMS-style dataset (write ~40 short messages inline in the code, clearly labelled as made-up, ham/spam), tokenization (reuse Day 6), word likelihoods with Laplace smoothing, log probabilities to avoid underflow, train/test split, accuracy and a confusion matrix, inspecting the most "spammy" words. Animated SVG summary. Next: `**Next:** Week 7 begins with Day 43: Samples, populations and the sampling distribution (coming soon)`.

## Week 7 lesson map: Statistics
- Day 43 `day-43-sampling.md` (~90 min): population vs sample, statistics vs parameters, sampling bias (survivorship, e.g. only reviewers who bother to rate), random sampling with rng from the Play Store data treated as a population, the sampling distribution of the mean, standard error σ/√n (figure: SE shrinks with n), sample variance with n−1 (Bessel's correction, shown by simulation). Next: Day 44: Confidence intervals and the bootstrap (coming soon).
- Day 44 `day-44-confidence-intervals.md` (~120 min): what a 95% CI means (and the common misreading), normal-approximation CI for a mean with a worked example, simulation showing ~95% of intervals cover the truth (animated SVG of intervals dropping in), the bootstrap (resample with replacement) for the median rating of a category and for a difference in means, CI for a proportion. Next: Day 45: Hypothesis tests, p-values and A/B tests (coming soon).
- Day 45 `day-45-hypothesis-tests.md` (~120 min): null and alternative, test statistic, p-value as "how surprising if nothing is going on", the permutation test properly (link to Day 20/27), the two-sample z/t test by formula and checked against permutation, significance level, type I/II errors, power and sample size intuition, multiple comparisons, p-hacking warning, A/B test framing. Android bridge: Firebase A/B Testing / Remote Config experiments. Next: Day 46: Correlation, causation and confounders (coming soon).
- Day 46 `day-46-correlation-causation.md` (~90 min): Pearson correlation recap, Spearman rank correlation, correlation ≠ causation with concrete confounders, Simpson's paradox with a real-looking worked table (and on the Play Store data if one exists, otherwise synthetic clearly labelled), randomized experiments as the fix, causal diagrams (Mermaid with quoted labels) for confounder/mediator/collider, spurious correlations from many comparisons. Next: Day 47: Maximum likelihood estimation (coming soon).
- Day 47 `day-47-maximum-likelihood.md` (~120 min): likelihood vs probability, MLE for a coin (derive p̂ = k/n with the log-likelihood derivative — uses Week 5 calculus), MLE for a normal's mean and variance, log-likelihood plots (figures), why minimizing MSE = MLE under Gaussian noise and why cross-entropy = MLE for classification (preview of Day 50/54), numerical MLE by grid search. Next: Day 48: Week 7 review and mini-project (coming soon).
- Day 48 `day-48-week7-review.md` (~3 h, review-day structure): recap of Days 43–47; MINI-PROJECT: analyse an A/B test of an app feature: a generated (seeded, clearly synthetic) experiment log of ~20,000 users with variant, sessions, conversion and revenue; check randomization balance, compute conversion lift with a CI (formula and bootstrap), a permutation test, a sample-size/power calculation for the next test, a pre-registered decision rule, and a one-page write-up template. Next: `**Next:** Week 8 begins with Day 50: Loss functions: what "wrong" means (coming soon)`.

## Week 8 lesson map: Optimization and information theory
- Day 50 `day-50-loss-functions.md` (~90 min): what a loss is, MSE vs MAE (and robustness to outliers, shown on the dataset), Huber, binary cross-entropy / log loss with a worked example, why accuracy isn't differentiable, loss surfaces for a 1-parameter and 2-parameter model (figures), the loss as a function of the parameters not the data. Next: Day 51: Gradient descent (coming soon).
- Day 51 `day-51-gradient-descent.md` (~120 min): the algorithm (w ← w − η∇L), 1-D then 2-D on a bowl, full linear regression on the Play Store data with the Day 33 gradient (standardize features, track loss per step, compare with np.linalg.lstsq), stopping criteria, the learning rate too small / right / too large (diverging) figures. WIDGET: `gd-playground` (docs/javascripts/gd-playground.js): a contour plot of a 2-D loss (selectable: round bowl, stretched bowl, a two-minima function) where clicking sets the start point and a learning-rate slider plus Step/Run/Reset buttons animate the GD path and show the loss value. Next: Day 52: Learning rates, mini-batches, SGD and momentum (coming soon).
- Day 52 `day-52-sgd-momentum.md` (~120 min): batch vs stochastic vs mini-batch gradient descent (noise vs speed, figures of loss curves), epochs and shuffling, momentum (ball rolling analogy, the update equations, the ravine example), a brief honest intro to Adam (equations, defaults), learning-rate schedules (step, cosine), feature scaling's effect on conditioning (link to Day 24 eigenvalues). Animated SVG comparing plain GD and momentum paths on a ravine. Next: Day 53: Convexity, local minima and saddle points (coming soon).
- Day 53 `day-53-convexity.md` (~90 min): convex sets and functions (chord above the curve), why convex losses (linear regression MSE, logistic loss) have one minimum, second derivative and curvature, local vs global minima, saddle points (z = x² − y², and why they dominate in high dimensions), plateaus, how GD behaves near each (figures), why deep learning works anyway (brief, honest). Next: Day 54: Entropy, cross-entropy and KL divergence (coming soon).
- Day 54 `day-54-information-theory.md` (~120 min): surprise = −log p, entropy as average surprise (coin, dice, a skewed distribution), bits vs nats, cross-entropy H(p, q) and why it's the classification loss, KL divergence = cross-entropy − entropy (not symmetric, show it), softmax + cross-entropy with a worked 3-class example and its gradient (p − y), connection to MLE (Day 47) and compression (Huffman intuition, in one paragraph). Animated SVG. Next: Days 55–56: Month 2 milestone (coming soon).
- Day 55 `day-55-month2-milestone.md` (covers Days 55–56; special structure like Day 27's page, title `# Days 55–56: Month 2 Milestone`): the milestone from docs/plan/month-02.md: linear regression on the Play Store data (choose target and features, standardize with train statistics only, train/validation/test split with a fixed seed), derive the MSE gradient by hand (show the derivation template), implement it, verify with grad_check, train with mini-batch GD, plot learning curves, beat a baseline (predict the training mean) by a stated margin on the test set, compare with np.linalg.lstsq; a rubric, a report template, a complete collapsed reference solution (run it and report its real numbers), a Month 2 retrospective template, and "what's next in Month 3" (classic ML; its milestone from docs/plan/monthly.md). One animated SVG. Next: `**Next:** Month 3: classic machine learning (coming soon)`.
